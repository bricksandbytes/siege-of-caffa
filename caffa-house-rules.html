Dungeons & Dragons: Streamlined

A simplified rulebook based on the 2014 v5.0 Open Game License (OGL)

Dungeons & Dragons (D&D) is a tabletop game where you describe what your character does, roll dice to see what happens, and tell a story together.

One person is the Game Master (GM). Everyone else plays a character. The GM describes the world. You describe your actions. Dice decide uncertain outcomes.

OGL Foundation

  • This manual streamlines the 2014 v5.0 Open Game License (OGL) rules system.
  • The OGL provides the core mechanical framework.
  • Modifications and additions build upon this foundational system.
  • Custom mechanics tailored to our campaign are introduced.

Complexity is removed without removing the fun.

Ability Scores to Modifiers Conversion

All statistics function as direct modifiers:

  • Except Hit Points and Speed, which remain standard values.
  • The modifier value has everything you need for rolls and checks.
  • The need to constantly reference ability scores is eliminated.
Modifier to Ability Score: (Modifier × 2) + 10
Ability Score to Modifier: (Ability Score - 10) / 2 (round down)

Quick Start: Make a Character (5 Minutes)

Step 1: Pick a Class

Choose one. This determines your abilities, hit points, and how you fight.

  • Martial classes focus on physical combat skills: Barbarian, Fighter, Monk, Paladin, Ranger, Rogue.
  • Caster classes rely on spellcasting: Bard, Cleric, Druid, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard.

Step 2: Assign Ability Modifiers

Assign these modifiers to your six abilities: +3, +2, +2, +1, +0, -1

Ability Used For
Strength (STR) Melee attacks, athletics, lifting, breaking things
Dexterity (DEX) Ranged attacks, stealth, dodging, initiative
Constitution (CON) Hit points, resisting poison, stamina
Intelligence (INT) Arcana, history, investigation, nature
Wisdom (WIS) Perception, insight, medicine, survival
Charisma (CHA) Persuasion, deception, performance, intimidation

Step 3: Calculate Core Combat Stats

  • Hit Points (HP): Starting HP depends on class + CON modifier.
  • Proficiency Bonus (PB): Starts at +2 for Level 1 characters.
  • Speed: Standard base speed is 30 feet unless specified otherwise.

Glossary of Terms

  • Advantage: Roll twice and use the higher result.
  • Disadvantage: Roll twice and use the lower result.
  • ATK (Attack Roll): The roll attackers make during combat.
  • DEF (Defense Roll): The roll defenders make to avoid damage.
  • Bonus Action: An extra action taken in a turn supporting your primary action.
  • Damage Die Roll (DDR): The die rolled for weapon or spell damage.
  • GM (Game Master): The person running the game and describing the world.
  • Guard: A creature protecting another creature (a Ward).
  • Ward: A creature being protected by a Guard.
  • HP (Hit Points): How much damage you can take before falling unconscious.
  • PB (Proficiency Bonus): A static number added to trained attacks and skills.
  • Reaction: A special action triggered by a specific event.
