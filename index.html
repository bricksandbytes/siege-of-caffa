Dungeons & Dragons (D&D) is a tabletop game where you describe what your character does, roll dice to see what happens, and tell a story together.

One person is the Game Master (GM). Everyone else plays a character. The GM describes the world. You describe your actions. Dice decide uncertain outcomes.

Plague Walkers of Caffa

* Lord of the Rings (LOTR) meets The Walking Dead (TWD) *


The Horde Returns.

Less than two years ... the Golden Horde has returned. And this time they intend to finish what they started.

Inside the walls of a city twice as large as Minas Tirith, the people shelter in defiance of the returning invaders.

Elven archers. Human paladins. Dwarven barbarians. Half-orc clerics. Halfling monks. Merchants fleeing the war. Families escaping the countryside. Traders from Tana. Sailors, craftsmen, soldiers, refugees, pilgrims, and opportunists. People from across the Sea of Zalpa, and the lands beyond. All seeking safety behind the walls.

Will you protect them?

Suddenly ... unholy screams shatter the deathly silence.

Plague Walkers in the city.

Already under immense pressure, will this be the breaking point?

How do you face three enemies at once?

Outside the walls waits The armies of the Golden Horde.

Inside the gates roam Plague Walkers deadly to the touch.

All around you awaits hunger, politics, disease, ambition, and opportunity ... targets for the Horde or the Walkers

And somewhere among the crowded streets, fortified walls, merchant houses, churches, synagogues, markets, warehouses, and refugee camps, history is waiting for you to act.

The Siege has returned. The Walkers have risen.
